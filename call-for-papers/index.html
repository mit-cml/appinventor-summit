Call for Papers

Share your work at the MIT App Inventor Global Education Summit 2026

Submission deadline: May 4, 2026 at 11:59 PM EDT

Overview

The MIT App Inventor Global Education Summit invites paper and poster submissions on computing and AI education. Presenters of accepted papers and posters must register for and present in-person at the Summit. Author(s) may submit more than one paper or poster for consideration by completing a separate form for each submission.

Chaired by Dr. Evan Patton (ewpatton@mit.edu) and Dr. Natalie Lao (natalie@appinventor.org).

Submissions are due by 11:59 PM EDT on May 4, 2026.

Main Call for Papers

Papers should be 2–4 pages, excluding references. Accepted papers will be presented at the Summit and published under the Creative Commons 4.0 International Share-Alike license. Submissions will be organized into two tracks:

Track 1: Research

  • Learning and education studies related to computational thinking, AI, and data science
  • Teaching practices, curriculum design, and classroom/program implementation
  • Novel assessment, feedback, and evaluation frameworks for computing and AI education
  • Addressing access and increased participation in computing education
  • Impacts of computing and AI education policies

Track 2: Innovative Applications

  • Computing projects addressing real-world challenges such as climate, sustainability, health, civic engagement, and biotechnology
  • AI tools for good, interactive projects, and examples of computational action
  • The future of C.S. education in an A.I. world

Main Call for Posters

Posters should be no larger than 3 ft × 4 ft in either portrait or landscape orientation. The minimum font sizes recommended for readability are 48pt for body text and 32pt for captions. Accepted posters are published under the Creative Commons 4.0 International Share-Alike license.

Topics

  • Case studies in computing, AI, and data science education
  • Curriculum examples and innovative teaching approaches
  • Assessment, feedback, and adaptive learning in computing education
  • Demonstrations of projects, apps, and AI-enhanced instruction and learning tools
  • Uses of computing for real-world problem solving and social impact
